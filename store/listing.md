# Order Up! Google Play listing

App name (30 max): Order Up!

Short description (80 max):
Cook, serve and build your diner one table at a time. Tap, drag and grow!

Full description (4000 max):
Run the busiest little diner in town, one burger at a time.

Drag anywhere to move your chef. Walk over a station to cook, carry the food to the counter and watch the coins fly. Every coin goes into building more: new tables, new stations, a soda fountain, a fryer, a sundae bar. Fill all fifteen spots at Corner Grill and a whole new place opens up on the beach.

WHAT YOU DO
- Cook burgers, fries, hot dogs, pizza, sodas and sundaes, each on its own station.
- Serve guests at the counter and at tables before their patience runs out.
- Serve fast to earn tips and chain orders into combos.
- Hire cooks, waiters and a counter runner so the restaurant keeps going while you build.
- Spend coins on upgrades: faster feet, bigger arms, a hotter kitchen and a tastier menu.

TWO PLACES TO RUN
- Corner Grill: a cosy town diner with burgers, fries, sodas and sundaes, and VIP guests who tip in gems.
- Beach Truck: a food truck on the sand serving hot dogs, pizza, sodas and sundaes at picnic tables.

MAKE IT YOURS
Earn gems from VIP guests and as you build, then spend them on hats, outfits, shoes and chef colours, or on a Rush Hour that doubles your cash.

PLAYS THE WAY YOU WANT
- One thumb, portrait, short sessions or long ones.
- Works offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.
- Your progress saves on your phone.
