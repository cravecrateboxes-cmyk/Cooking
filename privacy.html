Order Up! Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Order Up! is a single-player restaurant game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game keeps

Your progress (coins, gems, what you have built, upgrades and the outfit you picked) and your sound and music settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or contact details.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No internet connection is used while you play.

Permissions

The Android app asks only for permission to vibrate, for small taps of feedback when you serve, collect and unlock things. You can turn vibration off in your phone's settings.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent to the developer email shown on the game's Google Play page.
